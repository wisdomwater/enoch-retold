## 44 - The Voice of Tranquillity

Walk in the paths of peace, my children, and hold these words where no thief can reach them—in the hidden chambers of your heart. I have seen how cunningly wickedness speaks, seldom naming itself by its true name. It comes offering advantage, security, wealth, vengeance, or power, and only afterward does a man discover what he has permitted to take root within him.

I saw men building upon such foundations. They raised houses through fraud and strengthened them through oppression, piling stone upon stone until their walls seemed immovable. Yet beneath their splendor I saw the foundations cracking, for no house can stand forever when the suffering of another has been mixed into its mortar.

Others gathered silver and gold until their chambers shone with it. Prosperity surrounded them like a wall, and because no enemy could easily enter, they imagined themselves secure. But they had forgotten the Most High in the days when their tables were full, and what they possessed slowly taught them the terrible illusion that they possessed themselves.

Do not misunderstand what I saw. Gold is not strong enough to corrupt a heart that remembers from whom all things come, nor is poverty itself righteousness. The danger begins when abundance whispers, *You need nothing beyond me*, and the soul begins drinking from its own cistern while forgetting the fountain from which every living water flows.

I saw the day when such illusions failed. Darkness came upon those who had trusted in their strength, and judgment reached foundations hidden beneath magnificent houses. What men had concealed was remembered, and what they had built by crushing the humble could no longer shelter them.

Then sorrow overwhelmed me.

O that my eyes had become clouds heavy with water, that I might have emptied them over the earth as rain. I had spoken terrible words concerning judgment, yet my heart did not become terrible with them. I wept because human beings fashioned for goodness had learned to hate one another, and because those capable of becoming light had chosen instead to deepen the darkness.

Remember this, my children, when you hear me speak against sinners. Do not imagine that righteousness requires you to delight in another's ruin. Hatred binds the one who carries it, and the person who repays evil with evil discovers too late that the poison prepared for an enemy has entered his own blood.

I saw false witnesses rise against the innocent. Their tongues became weapons, and they increased wickedness while believing that words vanished once spoken. But heaven remembers what earth forgets, and every lie by which a neighbor is wounded leaves its testimony before the One who judges without deception.

I saw also the powerful striking down righteousness simply because they possessed the strength to do so. They ate the finest grain and drank deeply from the springs while the humble were pressed beneath their feet. Their abundance made them resemble the blessed from a distance, but within them another witness spoke, for the heart knows when prosperity has been purchased with another person's suffering.

To these I pronounced woe, and the word was heavy upon my tongue.

But to those who suffered beneath them I spoke differently: Wait in hope.

I saw the righteous lifted like eagles above the terror that had pursued them. I saw them finding refuge beyond the reach of those who troubled them, hidden as small creatures disappear among the clefts of the rocks. What had seemed weak endured, while what had seemed unconquerable began to wither.

Then I saw light.

It gathered around those who had been oppressed, splendid and clear, until the shadows that had followed them could no longer remain. Restoration came near to them, not merely as the overthrow of their enemies, but as the return of what violence had tried to steal—their dignity, their peace, their place beneath heaven.

And from above I heard something I wish I could place within your hearing exactly as it came to mine.

It was the voice of tranquillity.

After so many visions of swords, accusations, burning houses, and trembling earth, that voice seemed stranger to me than thunder. It did not need to shout. Peace possesses a strength unlike the strength of oppressors, for oppression must continually defend itself, while peace rests in what is true.

Therefore, when the wicked trouble you, do not allow them to choose the shape of your soul. If they are cruel, you need not become cruel to prove that cruelty is wrong. If they lie, remain truthful; if they grasp, keep your hands open; if they hate, guard carefully the small flame within you, lest their darkness become yours.

Judgment belongs to the Holy One because only He sees every foundation.

You, my children, walk the path of peace.

There will be days when that path appears foolish beside the roads of those who prosper through violence. Yet I have seen where those roads end. I have watched wealth wither, strongholds fall, and power loosen its grip.

And beyond their noise, I have heard what remains.

The voice of tranquillity from heaven.
