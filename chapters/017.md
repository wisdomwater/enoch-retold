## 17 - The Fountain That Never Fails

The prayer of the righteous continued to rise from the earth, and the holy ones above the heavens gathered because of it. Their voices became one voice before the Lord of Spirits, praising Him while pleading that the blood poured into the earth would not be forgotten. They asked that the cries of the afflicted might remain before Him until justice came, and that divine patience toward those who devoured the weak would not permit violence to endure forever.

Then I saw the Ancient of Days seated upon the throne of His glory. Before Him the book of the living was opened, and the powers above the heavens gathered around Him in silence. Nothing that had happened beneath the sun was absent from that book; what kings had concealed beneath stone and what murderers imagined the earth had swallowed were present before Him.

And the hearts of the holy ones filled with joy.

Their joy was not because suffering had come upon others. It was because righteousness had reached its appointed hour. The prayers that had seemed unanswered were heard, and the blood that had seemed wasted was precious before the Lord of Spirits.

Then my eyes were drawn to a fountain.

It was a fountain of righteousness, and its waters never failed. Around it flowed many springs of wisdom, clear and abundant, and all who thirsted came to drink. They bent over those waters empty and rose from them filled with wisdom, dwelling thereafter among the righteous, the elect, and the holy.

I had crossed valleys where waters never diminished, but this fountain seemed deeper than all of them. I thought of the thirst that drives the human heart—the thirst to know, to possess, to be secure, to become important in the eyes of others. Men have drunk deeply from such desires and found themselves thirstier than before, yet here was water that satisfied because its source was righteousness.

Near that fountain I heard again of the Son of Man.

His name was spoken before the Lord of Spirits and before the Ancient of Days. I was told something I could scarcely comprehend: before the sun had burned in the heavens, before signs were appointed and before the stars were formed, his name was already known in the presence of God. The Elect One, the Concealed One, belonged to a mystery older than the world.

He would become a support for the righteous, something upon which the weary could lean without falling.

He would be the light of the nations.

And he would be the hope of troubled hearts.

Those words remained with me more deeply than many of the wonders I had seen. I knew troubled hearts. I had watched people live beneath rulers who could destroy them with a word, mothers grieve children taken by violence, and the righteous wonder whether their faithfulness mattered in a world where cruelty often prospered. Yet I was shown that before their trouble existed, hope had already been prepared.

Before the darkness, the light was known.

The rulers who had gained the world through the works of their hands would eventually stand before this Elect One. Their riches would not strengthen them there, nor would the monuments they raised preserve their names. I saw their power collapse like dry grass thrown into flame and like heavy lead disappearing beneath deep water.

The images were terrible, and I did not look upon them lightly.

But then I saw what followed.

Rest came upon the earth.

This changed how I understood the severity of the vision. The ending of oppression was also the beginning of tranquility. When the hand crushing a throat is removed, the one beneath it can breathe; when the weapon is broken, the village can sleep.

Iniquity itself passed like a shadow.

It had seemed so permanent while standing beneath it. Yet before the Elect One it possessed no fixed dwelling, no everlasting substance. Wisdom, by contrast, poured forth like water, and glory did not fail.

With the Elect One dwelt the spirit of wisdom, instruction, and power. Hidden things were opened before him, and secrets could no longer protect those who depended upon secrecy. The light reached even into the chambers where human beings hide from themselves.

Then I saw the chosen undergo a change.

The light of day rested upon them, and their splendour became something new. Those who had carried light quietly through a dark age now seemed clothed in the very brightness they had refused to abandon.

But I was also shown something unexpected among those who had walked in darkness.

Some were made to see.

They saw that the works of their own hands could not save them. They saw the emptiness of the glory they had pursued and understood that the road they traveled ended far from life. And before the finality of judgment, repentance stood before them like an open door.

I heard that by the name of the Lord of Spirits they might yet be saved.

For great is His mercy.

I do not tell you this so that darkness may be treated lightly. The same vision warned that the one who continually refuses repentance finally clings to what is passing away. But I learned that judgment and mercy were not strangers standing on opposite sides of heaven; righteousness itself exposed what destroys us, while mercy called us to release it.

Then the earth trembled with another mystery.

I saw the earth open her womb and surrender what she had received. The realm of the dead also gave back those entrusted to it, and even destruction was commanded to restore what it owed. Graves that had seemed final were no longer final.

The dead were not forgotten.

The righteous and holy were gathered, for the day of their salvation had drawn near. The Elect One sat upon his throne, and from his mouth came the secrets of wisdom given to him by the Lord of Spirits.

I stood there thinking of all that humanity calls irreversible.

Blood spilled.

Years lost.

Bodies buried.

Lives crushed beneath the ambitions of others.

Yet I had seen the earth commanded to give back what it had swallowed.

So when you encounter something within yourself or within this wounded world that seems beyond restoration, be careful before calling it lost forever. I have traveled farther than the graves of men, and I tell you that even there I found no kingdom beyond the reach of the Lord of Spirits.

There is a fountain that never fails.

There is mercy even in the hour when hidden things are revealed.

There is an Elect One who is called the hope of troubled hearts.

And I saw the earth open at the command of God and return what death believed belonged to it.
