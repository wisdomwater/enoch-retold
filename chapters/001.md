## 1 - When the Mountains Melt

I opened my eyes, and the heavens were before me.

There are things above us that remain hidden from ordinary sight, movements beyond the veil that we do not perceive as we go about our days beneath the sun. But the angels showed them to me, and I heard their voices. I understood that what I was seeing belonged not only to my generation, but to generations still sleeping in the womb of time.

I saw the Holy and Mighty One rise from his dwelling, and the hosts of heaven surrounded him. His presence moved across creation like fire across dry grass, and even the Watchers trembled before him. Those ancient ones who had gazed upon mysteries hidden from humanity suddenly became afraid.

Then the earth began to shake.

The mountains trembled beneath the weight of his coming. Great peaks that had stood since before the memory of our fathers began to move, and the high hills sank beneath them. Before the presence of the Holy One they melted like honeycomb held over a flame, and I understood how little permanence there is in the things we call permanent.

Remember this when you find yourself afraid of the powers of this world. There will always be mountains that appear immovable, rulers who seem beyond challenge, violence that seems impossible to overcome, and darkness so old that you may mistake it for part of creation itself. Do not confuse what has endured for a long time with what will endure forever.

I saw judgment move across the earth, and I confess that the sight frightened me. Yet the angels had called what I received a blessing, and slowly I began to understand why. There are things in this world whose ending is an act of mercy.

Cruelty must have an ending. Violence must have an ending. The strong cannot forever devour the weak while heaven remains silent, and every kingdom built upon fear will eventually discover that even mountains can melt.

As I watched the hills collapse, I wondered about the mountains hidden within the human heart. We build them slowly from anger, fear, envy, pride, and the desire to control what cannot be controlled. Stone by stone we raise them until we can no longer remember what the land looked like before they were there.

Do not fear when the light begins to melt them. The fire of God does not come only to destroy; light also reveals what darkness has concealed. Sometimes what we experience as judgment is simply truth entering a place where illusion has lived too long.

For in the midst of everything I saw—the trembling Watchers, the shaking earth, the mountains flowing like wax—I heard a word I did not expect. It came quietly against the thunder of judgment, almost too gentle for such a terrible vision. The word was **peace**.

The Holy One would preserve those who remained faithful to the light. Mercy would surround them, and when the shaking had passed, the splendor of God would illuminate them. The vision that began in terror ended not in darkness, but in radiance.

So when you find yourself living in troubled days, do not believe that you must become like the darkness in order to survive it. Hatred does not need more hatred, nor does violence need another hand willing to wield it. Become instead a keeper of whatever light has been entrusted to you.

Your flame may seem very small against such darkness, but carry it anyway. Be merciful when others become cruel, remain gentle when others mistake hardness for strength, and tell the truth without hatred. Protect those placed within your reach without surrendering your own heart to fear.

There are powers in this world larger than you, just as there were powers in mine. There are things you cannot command, battles you cannot win, and mysteries you will never understand. You were never asked to hold the whole world together; you were asked only to remain faithful to the light you have been given.

I have seen beyond the night. I have watched the ancient ones tremble and mountains bow before the Holy One. I have learned that darkness possesses far less permanence than it pretends to possess.

So walk toward the light, even while the mountains shake.

For when everything that cannot endure has finally fallen away, it is not darkness that covers creation.

It is the splendor of God.
