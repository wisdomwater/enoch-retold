# Enoch Retold

*Enoch Retold* is a contemplative literary retelling of *1 Enoch*, told in the first-person voice of its ancient seer. Fifty readings journey through the Watchers, heavenly realms, judgment, the movements of stars and seasons, and visions of restoration. The book asks how to walk through a world of real darkness without becoming dark oneself, inviting courage, integrity, mercy, and hope. It is a modern literary adaptation rather than a new translation.
